Studio Monograph

Objects for everyday living.

NOVA SUPPLY was established on a single foundational premise: that the physical instruments we touch each day should possess tactile honesty, acoustic calm, and structural permanence.

01 / Reductive Form

Modern consumer products shout for attention through glossy plastic, RGB LEDs, and excessive surface complexity. We take the opposing path: resolving tolerances until nothing non-essential remains.

02 / Honest Metallurgy

We machine our desk risers from continuous aerospace aluminium, weave our throws from Flemish flax, and cast kettles using centuries-old Nanbu tekki metallurgy.
